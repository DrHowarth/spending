# Spending — Barry's personal spending tracker

Read `HANDOVER.md` first (state, stamp, queue), then `ARCHITECTURE.md`. Barry's working method is the
playbook in his simulators repo (`_Working Practices/PLAYBOOK.md`): two gates, small passes, one feature per session.

**This repo is public. Never put bank data in it** — no statements, no `spending-data.json`, and no real
figures, payees or names in tests, docs or commit messages. Real samples and their expected figures live in
Barry's data folder (`~/Documents/Bank Things/` on the iMac). The pre-commit hook is a backstop, not the rule.
